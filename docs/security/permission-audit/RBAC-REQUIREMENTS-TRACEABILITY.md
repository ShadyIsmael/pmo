## Completeness and rating reconciliation

- The located source has **83 original rows: 39 Direct + 44 Corner**. It explicitly says enumerated, not completeness-certified. The Requirements Analyst and Workflow Auditor mapped every original ID, but do not claim full product/API/workflow coverage from this count.
- The 29 RBC entries are supplemental reconstructions crosswalked to original IDs, not 29 validated source rows and not 29 proven-new scenarios. The matrix proposes 16 unique additions for PO review.
- Preserve original W/S values. EP-03's Direct classification conflicts with the source's own definition of Corner for negative/security cases; propose reclassification while preserving its ID and W5/S0. UR-03 stays W5/S1; synthetic RBC-D02 S0 is not an original rating change.
- Source GAP-01..12 remain open until each is added or explicitly marked Not Applicable with evidence.
- High-risk coverage includes the PM assignment journey, System-role invariant, Organization Admin scope, Protected Core/Active Deny, cross-scope checks, last-admin protection, and concurrency.
