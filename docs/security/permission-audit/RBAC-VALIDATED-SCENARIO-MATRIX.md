## Source matrix and interpretation

The source was located in conversation **Sources** as `Pasted markdown(9).md`, titled **“FOPX PM — Roles & Permissions: Final Goal, Governance, Business Journeys & Scenario Coverage”**, prepared 2026-11-14 (Library item `libfile_beac8302215c8191a2b500d789d12a04`). Its §5 enumerates 83 original IDs across RM, UR, DP, PA, EP, UX, and CM. Those IDs, classifications, and original Weight/Severity values are preserved below. The attachment explicitly says it is an enumerated Council draft, not a completeness certification or PO approval.

The previously created 29 `RBC-*` entries are retained later as supplemental reconstructed candidates and crosswalked to the source IDs. They are **not** the source matrix, not 29 proven-new requirements, and are not added to the 83 count. Newly proposed scenarios are separately numbered in the final report and remain unapproved.

`Confirmed` means the requirement is explicitly binding in the active PO instruction or an established project decision; it does **not** mean implementation passed or the entire scenario is approved. `Conflicting` identifies a conflict with an explicit PO/established rule or a taxonomy/rating inconsistency. `Proposed` and `Requires PO Decision` are not approved requirements. Runtime, database, and test evidence are tracked separately from scenario status.

For original source ratings, preserve the source rubric: **Weight 5** = critical/core journey; 4 high; 3 material; 2 moderate; 1 limited. **Severity S0** = critical compromise, lockout, or cross-Tenant impact; S1 = high core-operation/access failure; S2 = moderate; S3 = low UI impact. Severity is impact if mishandled, not release priority. Original W/S values remain unchanged pending PO review. One source classification issue is proposed for correction: EP-03 is Direct although the source's own Type rubric places negative/security cases under Corner; preserve EP-03 and record the proposed reclassification rather than silently changing it.
